# Dwak (دواك)

register: product

## Product Purpose
Dwak answers one question under stress: "Which pharmacy near me actually has my medicine, right now?" Algeria has a chronic medicine shortage (52+ molecules in rupture per SNAPO, 2026); 42% of patients visit 3+ pharmacies to find a treatment. Dwak crowdsources and verifies availability across pharmacies, suggests generic substitutes when a medicine is out of stock, locates the pharmacie de garde at night/weekends, and alerts patients when their medicine is back.

## Users
- Patients and caregivers in Algerian cities (Algiers, Oran, Constantine first). Often searching on foot or in a car, outdoors, in daylight glare, urgently, for a parent or child. Phone-first, mid-range Android, patchy 4G.
- Pharmacists (secondary): confirm/deny stock, gain foot traffic. Need a 10-second update flow, not a dashboard chore.

## Language & Locale
French primary, Arabic (RTL) full parity. Darija-flavored tone where warmth helps ("Dwak" = "your medicine"). Dinar, DZ phone formats, wilaya/commune geography.

## Tone
Calm competence under urgency. A capable pharmacist who knows the neighborhood: direct, warm, zero bureaucracy. Never alarmist, never cutesy. Health claims stay sober and sourced.

## Anti-references
- White + teal "healthcare SaaS" template. Sterile hospital UI.
- Pharmacy-cross neon green clichés.
- Yassir/delivery-app gradient-purple super-app look.
- Government portal density (DZair Digital Services). Facebook-group chaos.

## Strategic principles
- Time-to-answer is the metric: search → verified pharmacy in under 15 seconds.
- Trust is the product: every availability claim shows freshness and source (pharmacist vs community), never overstates certainty.
- Works in the street: readable in sunlight, thumb-reachable, tolerant of bad connectivity (PWA offline shell, cached last results).
- Legality: never sells or reserves prescription medicine online (law 18-05); it only locates licensed pharmacies.
